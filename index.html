THE FIELD JOURNAL

Every move has a purpose.

Hold back or Space to block mid and overhead attacks. Add S to block low attacks; overheads beat low guard. Throws beat guarding and miss airborne opponents. Light → heavy → special can combo on hit. Three gold stocks unlock a super; its opening strike must connect. Double-tap toward your opponent to dash (A A or D D on keyboard, the stick or D-pad on touch and controller) and away to backdash.

PHONE / TWO THUMBS

Use landscape for the largest view. Left stick: move, up to jump, down to crouch; flick it toward or away twice to dash or backdash. Right buttons match the keyboard's six actions. Hold down on the stick for sweep (Light), overhead (Heavy), low special (Special), or counter/anti-air (Technique). Hold Guard + down + Special for Sayak's Ancestor. Guard and Throw sit beside the stick. These controls appear on touch devices and stay visible in fullscreen.

CONTROLLER / STANDARD XBOX OR PLAYSTATION LAYOUT

Connect over USB or Bluetooth, then press a controller button. D-pad / left stick moves; up jumps, down crouches; double-tap toward or away to dash or backdash. X / Square: Light · A / Cross: Heavy · B / Circle: Special · Y / Triangle: Throw · LB / L1: Technique · RB / R1: Awaken · LT / L2: Guard · RT / R2: Super · Start / Options: Play or pause. Hold down to use the same directional variants as the keyboard; down + LT/L2 + B/Circle calls Ancestor. First detected controller is P1; second is P2 in Local two-player. Unknown layouts are reported rather than guessed.

PLAYER TWO / KEYBOARD

Arrow keys move, jump and crouch; double-tap ← or → to dash or backdash. Enter guards. Num 1/2/3: Light / Heavy / Special. Num 4/5/6: Technique / Awaken / Super. Num 0: Throw. Hold down for directional variants; Decimal calls Ancestor. A second controller also works.
