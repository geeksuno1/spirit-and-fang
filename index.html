THE FIELD JOURNAL

Every move has a purpose.

Block by holding back or Guard: a standing block stops mid and overhead attacks, a crouching block (hold down) stops lows. Throws beat blocking but miss airborne opponents. Light → heavy → special can combo on hit. Three gold stocks unlock a super; its opening strike must connect.
